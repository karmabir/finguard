# P-02 Account-emptying pattern
SYNTHETIC POLICY. A transaction whose amount is approximately equal to the origin balance (amount between 95% and 100% of oldbalanceOrg, with oldbalanceOrg greater than 0), especially via TRANSFER or CASH_OUT, drains the account and is a strong fraud indicator: escalate.
If amount is greater than oldbalanceOrg, or oldbalanceOrg is 0, the balance fields are inconsistent. That is a data-quality note, not an account-emptying signal; do not treat it as a drain.
