# P-08 False-positive guidance
SYNTHETIC POLICY. Large amounts alone are not proof of fraud. If a high-value transaction is well covered by the origin balance (amount well below oldbalanceOrg) and shows no other indicator, mark it review rather than escalate.

Clear criteria for TRANSFER or CASH_OUT: choose clear when the amount is not approximately equal to the origin balance (P-02 does not apply), no velocity alert (P-05) or round-trip chain (P-06) is found in the data, and the destination is not a pattern of repeated drained deposits. Unusual-looking balances or large amounts alone are not enough to escalate.
